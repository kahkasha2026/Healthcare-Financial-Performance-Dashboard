Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the financial performance of a healthcare center using a healthcare dataset. The objective is to uncover meaningful business insights, identify key financial trends, evaluate healthcare provider performance, and present the findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders make data-driven decisions by providing a clear understanding of:

Financial health of the healthcare center
Revenue and cost trends
Provider efficiency and performance
Operational insights and business trends
Patient and billing patterns
Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

Perform detailed financial analysis of the healthcare center
Identify important KPIs and business trends
Analyze healthcare provider performance and efficiency
Build an interactive and user-friendly Power BI dashboard
Present actionable insights for decision-making

Business Insights & Findings

Some important findings from the analysis include:

Certain departments contribute significantly higher revenue compared to others.
A small group of healthcare providers generated the majority of profits.
Seasonal patterns were observed in patient admissions and revenue.
Insurance-based payments formed a major portion of total collections.
Some providers handled high patient volume but showed lower profitability.
Operational expenses increased steadily over time, impacting overall margins.
Project Outcome

This dashboard helps healthcare stakeholders:

Monitor financial health effectively
Identify top-performing healthcare providers
Track operational and financial trends
Improve strategic decision-making
Optimize resource allocation
Enhance overall business performance
